About Us

Static Power Systems is a trusted manufacturer of Online UPS systems designed for critical applications where power continuity is essential. With decades of experience, we've built our reputation on engineering excellence, reliability, and customer satisfaction.

Our XP Series Online UPS systems provide unmatched protection for:

  • Industrial Loads
  • Medical Equipment
  • IT / ITES Infrastructure
  • Other Critical Loads

We pride ourselves on delivering not just products, but complete power protection solutions tailored to your specific requirements.

Advanced Features

Our XP Series UPS systems are engineered with cutting-edge technology

16 Bit DSP Control

Advanced digital signal processing for superior performance and reliability

Inbuilt Isolation Transformer

Complete galvanic isolation for enhanced protection against electrical noise

Inbuilt Static Switch

Seamless power transfer between sources with zero interruption

Inbuilt Manual Bypass Switch

Allows maintenance without power interruption to critical loads

100% Unbalanced Load Support

Maintains stable output even with severely unbalanced loads

Output Voltage Regulation <1%

Precise voltage regulation for sensitive equipment protection

Rectifier Current Limit Function

Prevents overload conditions and ensures system stability
